# Go2 ActiveVLN demo media

Public presentation assets for EmbodiRun v0.1. The original recordings remain outside this repository.

- `go2_activevln_3x.mp4`: 9 seconds at 3× playback speed.
- `go2_activevln_3x.jpg`: poster extracted from the published MP4.
- The three cases are `--mode chunk --max-chunks 1`, `--mode chunk --max-chunks 100`, and `--mode overlap --max-chunks 100 --overlap-lead-s 1.5`.
- RTC footage shows the first recorded run; the later manual head-return sequence is excluded.
- The timing illustration is a static reconstruction of that run's log, not synchronized live telemetry. Its 1.493 s overlap and 1.861 s boundary wait are observations from one run, not a controlled speed comparison.

The silent H.264 exports contain re-typeset commands; no credentials, deployment addresses, checkpoints or original logs are published.
